   at the Playwright route level. New stateful mocks make creates feel

   "Expenses & Cash Control" (expenses → reconciliations) and "Sunday
   Payouts" (payouts → commission), plus Reports as its own scene — it's

   runtime ("AS LONG AS IT NEEDS"), so nothing was dropped or thinned.
   Trim by removing scenes from the manifest / `CUTAWAYS` in
   `apps/demo/remotion/timeline.ts` if a shorter cut is ever wanted.

   weekday off).

    the redirect to login.
